# Duo StandBy: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 14, 2026_

Duo StandBy is made by Ari Berkowitz ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app and its widgets.

## The short version

**We don't collect any data.** No account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.

## What stays on your device

- **Your settings** (colors, fonts, name tags, countdown) are saved only on your iPhone, inside the app and its widgets.
- **The photo** you choose for the Photo Clock widget is resized and saved only on your iPhone. It is never uploaded.
- **Name tags and signs** you type are saved only on your iPhone.

We can't see any of this, and it is deleted when you delete the app.

## Purchases

In-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the app which packs you own so it can unlock them.

## Children

The app doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
