## Description
Make StandBy, your Home Screen and your Lock Screen look amazing.

CLOCKS FOR STANDBY
• 15 clock styles: Flip, Minimal, Analog, Bold, Neon, LCD, Terminal, Synthwave, Galaxy, Aurora, Nebula, Orbit, Word Clock, Binary and Studio
• Every style is its own widget, in small, medium and large
• Pick your accent color, font and 12 or 24-hour time

LIVE SCENES
• The classic DVD bounce. Wait for the corner!
• Matrix rain, warp-speed starfield and Mystify
• Cozy fireplace, a flickering candle and a lava lamp
• Add the Live Scene widget: one tap opens the scene full screen, even from StandBy

NAME TAGS AND SIGNS
• Fold your phone like a tent or stand it up and show your name and title
• 16 ready signs: Do Not Disturb, Busy, On a Call, Lunch Break, Recording and more
• Your own colors and saved presets
• Smart signs: LED scroller, talk timer and "Now Serving" counter

MORE WIDGETS
• Calendar with a month view, Moon Phase, Countdown, Quote of the hour and Photo Clock
• Lock Screen widgets for Countdown, Moon, Name Tag and Quote

FAIR PRICING
• Lots of styles are free forever
• Unlock packs once and keep them: Retro, Space, Pro and Live, or get All Access
• No subscriptions, no ads, no tracking

## App Review notes
Thank you for reviewing Duo StandBy.

- Widgets: long-press the Home Screen > Edit > Add Widget > "Duo StandBy". Every clock style is its own widget. "Clock: Any Style" switches styles via Edit Widget.
- StandBy: charge the device in landscape and add the widgets to the StandBy widget stack.
- Live Scene widget: widgets can't animate, so tapping it opens the chosen scene full screen in the app.
- In-app purchases (non-consumable): Store tab. Retro Pack, Space Pack, Pro Pack and Live Pack unlock styles; All Access unlocks everything. Restore Purchases is at the bottom of the Store tab.
- Locked styles appear blurred with a lock; tapping them opens the Store.
- No account or login is needed. The app collects no data.
