## Common questions

**How do I add a widget?**
Long-press your Home Screen, tap **Edit**, then **Add Widget**, and search for **Duo StandBy**. Every clock style is its own widget.

**How do I use StandBy?**
Charge your iPhone, turn it sideways and let it rest. Swipe to the widgets screen, long-press a widget stack, tap **+** and add Duo StandBy.

**Why don't widgets move?**
Apple doesn't allow animated widgets. Add the **Live Scene** widget: tapping it opens the animated scene full screen.

**I bought a pack but it's still locked.**
Open the app, go to **Store** and tap **Restore Purchases**. Packs are one-time purchases and are never charged twice.

**My widget didn't update after I changed a setting.**
Widgets refresh on Apple's schedule. Remove and add the widget again, or restart your iPhone.

**Do you collect my data?**
No. See the Privacy Policy.
